Hues & Cues

A colour-guessing party game for your Twitch stream. You pick a secret colour and give a one-word clue; chat guesses the square by typing coordinates like F12.

Create an account Sign in
  1. Create an account, then enter your Twitch channel name.
  2. Copy the overlay URL into an OBS Browser Source (1920 × 1080).
  3. Draw a card, pick a colour, give a clue — chat does the rest.
